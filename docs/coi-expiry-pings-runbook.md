# Owner COI expiry pings: switch, limits and rollback (2026-10-21)

What it is: the notification worker emails the Demohub owner at 30, 14 and 3 days before a brand's approved certificate of insurance (brands.default_coi_expires) lapses, while that brand has paid demos dated today or later. The owner panel's COI Review tab lists the same brands under "Expiring in the next 30 days" with the recorded state of each ping. Migration 0093 lets these brand-scoped delivery rows exist without a store attached.

## The switch: COI_EXPIRY_PINGS_ENABLED

- Exactly the string `true` means on. Unset, empty, `TRUE`, padded or anything else means off (api/_flags.js, read at request time).
- Set it in Vercel on Production only, and redeploy after changing it. Leave it unset on Preview unless a contained-recipient test is deliberately being run there.
- Off means: the sweep writes no rows and reports `enabled:false` plus `held` (how many COI rows are waiting) in the worker heartbeat; the claim filter excludes the kind, so pending, failed, unknown and frozen-retry COI rows are neither claimed nor sent and never occupy a slot other kinds need; a COI row that still reaches a worker (claimed just before the switch) is put back to pending untouched, outcome `deferred`; the owner panel shows the switch-off notice and reads pending rows as held. Every other notification kind is unaffected.
- On again means: held rows resume under their normal windows. A row whose 24-hour catch-up window passed while off is recorded skipped `expired` and never sent late. Dedupe keys are unchanged, so the next sweep adds nothing for rows that already exist. A frozen retry replays its frozen body under its original provider key.
- Limits: a send already handed to the mail provider cannot be recalled. The switch takes effect at the next deploy, so a worker run already in flight finishes on the old value.

## What the schedule does not do (deliberate for the pilot)

- The 24-hour catch-up applies to rows already scheduled. A brand first qualifying after 09:00 Pacific on an offset day has that offset recorded skipped `due_before_scheduling`, even inside 24 hours.
- A brand first qualifying under three days before expiry gets no email from this three-offset schedule. It still appears in the panel while within 30 days.
- The sweep looks 31 days ahead to prepare the 30-day row a day early; the panel shows 30 days. The two lists differ at that boundary by design.
- "Upcoming" is date-based on the owner's calendar: a paid demo dated today counts even if its start time has passed.
- Reads are capped at 500 brands, 1,000 bookings and 1,000 ping rows. A read that comes back full is reported as truncated: the sweep flags the run (`coi_expiry:truncated`, a failed heartbeat) and the panel shows "may be incomplete". Pagination is a follow-up before the pilot grows anywhere near those numbers.

## Rollback

Prefer the switch: unset COI_EXPIRY_PINGS_ENABLED on Production and redeploy. The kind-aware code stays deployed, queued rows are held, nothing is deleted or marked sent by hand.

Rolling back to a build that does not know the kind is not the ordinary stop button: such a build's dispatcher may claim a due COI row and record it skipped `unknown_kind` for good. Before any such rollback: switch the feature off first (so nothing new is queued), then either wait until no COI row is pending, failed or unknown (query below) or accept that those rows are retired. Migration 0093 stays in place in every case; it is a widening of a CHECK constraint and harms no other kind.

```sql
select status, count(*) from notification_deliveries where kind = 'coi_expiry_owner' group by status order by status;
```

2. The guarded 0093 kit (generated by tools/make-0093-paste.mjs, rehearsed by tools/rehearse-0093-paste.mjs on demohub-rebuild-check inside a rolled-back transaction). Paste first.
3. Deploy the integrated build with the switch unset. Verify build identity, the owner panel (switch-off notice visible, list correct) and the next worker heartbeat (`coi_expiry: { enabled: false, held: 0 }`).
4. Set COI_EXPIRY_PINGS_ENABLED=true on Production, redeploy. Verify the next heartbeat's `coi_expiry` counts against the preflight prediction and the panel's per-ping states.
