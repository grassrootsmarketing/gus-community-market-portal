Pricing questions.

Is it really free for retailers?

Yes. There is no subscription, no setup fee and no per-demo charge to the store, at any number of locations. Demohub is paid by the $10 booking fee the brand pays when it books.

What counts as a "demo"?

A confirmed booking in your Demohub admin. Pending, declined and cancelled bookings don't count, and the brand's $10 fee follows your cancellation policy like the rest of the payment.

What if I don't want to charge brands a demo fee?

Then don't. Set your demo fee to $0 and the brand pays only the $10 Demohub fee. Plenty of stores run demos free and use Demohub for the calendar, the SKUs and the insurance tracking. You can add a fee later, per location.

If I do set a demo fee, how do I get paid?

Connect your bank in Settings, Bank and payouts, which uses Stripe Express. The brand pays at booking, your fee is routed to your Stripe account, and it pays out on your Stripe schedule. A monthly statement in the admin lists every demo and what it earned.

Do brands pay anything else?

No. Brands pay the flat $10 booking fee per confirmed demo plus whatever demo fee the retailer charges. There is no subscription and no account cost. They get a profile, a dashboard and one-click booking at every Demohub retailer.

How many locations and team members can I add?

As many as you have. Locations, admins, viewers and store contacts are all unlimited, and each location keeps its own hours, lead time, fee and contacts.
