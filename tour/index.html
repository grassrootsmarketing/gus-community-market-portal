A 30 second look at Demohub, which is free for stores.

Free for your store

There is no subscription and no charge to the store, at any number of locations. The brand pays a flat $10 to book.

Insurance comes first

A brand cannot book a demo until its certificate of insurance is on file, so nobody at your store chases paperwork.

One calendar for your team

Every confirmed demo shows on your admin calendar, with the brand, the location and the time.
